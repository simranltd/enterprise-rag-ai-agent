## 1. Purpose

This policy defines reasonable business expenses, approval requirements, and
reimbursement timelines for Northstar employees. Expenses must have a
business purpose, be incurred economically, and be supported by an itemized
receipt.

## 2. Submission deadline

Employees must submit an expense report within **30 calendar days** after the
expense date. A manager may approve a late submission only when the employee
documents the reason for the delay.

## 3. Approval thresholds

| Expense total before tax | Required approval |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Employee's manager |
| $501 to $5,000 | Employee's manager and department budget owner |
| Above $5,000 | Department head and Finance Operations |

Expenses above **$5,000 CAD** require written approval from both the
department head and Finance Operations before the commitment is made. Splitting
one purchase into multiple reports to avoid a threshold is prohibited.

## 4. Travel and hospitality

Air travel should use economy class unless the Chief Operating Officer grants
an exception for a documented medical or operational reason. Client
hospitality must identify the business purpose and attendees. Alcohol is
reimbursable only when part of a reasonable client meal and permitted by the
department budget owner.

## 5. Restricted expenses

Personal purchases, traffic or parking fines, home internet upgrades without
prior approval, and expenses that violate law or a client agreement are not
reimbursable. Finance may request additional evidence before payment.

## 6. Exceptions and disputes

Finance Operations may approve a documented exception. Employees who disagree
with a rejection should first contact the reviewing Finance analyst, followed
by the Finance manager if the issue is unresolved.

## 7. Superseded version

Version 2.4, effective 2024-09-01, previously required Finance Operations
approval for expenses above $10,000. Version 2.4 is **superseded** and must
not be used for current expense decisions. Version 3.0 lowered the current
threshold to above $5,000 CAD.
