## 1. Access principles

Access is granted according to least privilege, business need, and separation
of duties. A manager request alone does not authorize access to restricted
systems; the system owner or designated approver must also approve it.

## 2. Role-specific approvals

| Access | Required approvers |
|---|---|
| Standard productivity tools | Manager |
| Client Operations case system | Manager and Client Operations system owner |
| Compliance case system | Manager and Director of Compliance |
| Production infrastructure | Manager, system owner, and Technology Security |
| Privileged administrator role | System owner, Technology Security, and CISO delegate |

Temporary privileged access must include a start and expiry time and may not
remain active longer than **24 hours** without a new approval.

## 3. Joiner, mover, and leaver controls

People Operations sends a start, transfer, or termination event through the
approved workflow. Technology provisions only approved access. Managers must
review a transfer's access and remove access no longer required.

For terminated employees, all interactive access must be revoked by the end
of the termination day, or immediately when People Operations marks the
termination as involuntary or security-sensitive. Tokens, sessions, badges,
and privileged credentials must also be invalidated as applicable.

## 4. Access reviews

System owners review privileged access monthly and standard access quarterly.
Access that cannot be justified is removed and the decision is recorded.

## 5. Lost credentials or devices

Users must report lost devices, suspected credential compromise, or unusual
login activity immediately to the Service Desk. Technology may suspend access
before completing a full investigation.

## 6. Exceptions

Exceptions require documented business justification, an expiry date, and
approval by Technology Security. Permanent exceptions are not permitted.
