## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes the minimum steps for onboarding a new institutional
or commercial client. It must be followed before services are activated.
Client Operations coordinates the process, while Compliance owns the
regulatory review.

## 2. Required identification

For an organization, the onboarding file must include:

1. Legal name and operating name, if different.
2. Certificate of incorporation or equivalent registry evidence.
3. Registered address and principal place of business.
4. Names and identification for directors and authorized signing officers.
5. Ownership information identifying individuals who ultimately own or control
   the organization.
6. A description of expected account activity and source of funds.

Individuals must provide a government-issued photo identification document,
address information, and tax residency information. Documents must be
current, legible, and verified through an approved process.

## 3. Workflow and approvals

The Client Operations analyst collects documents and records the checklist.
The relationship manager confirms the commercial rationale. Compliance
performs the identity, sanctions, and risk review. A client may be activated
only after Compliance records an approval and the required agreement is
executed.

High-risk classifications require enhanced due diligence under the AML
Procedure and approval from the Director of Compliance before activation.

## 4. Missing or inconsistent information

Do not activate a client with missing mandatory identification. Escalate
inconsistent ownership, unexplained source of funds, or suspected
misrepresentation to Compliance. Do not tell a client that a suspicious
activity report has been or may be filed.

## 5. Record retention

Client Operations stores the completed checklist and evidence in the approved
case system. Access is limited to staff with an onboarding, compliance, legal,
or audit need. Retention follows the Records Retention Schedule.
