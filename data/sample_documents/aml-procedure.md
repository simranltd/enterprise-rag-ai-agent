## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines Northstar's fictional minimum controls for identifying
clients, assessing risk, monitoring activity, and escalating concerns. It is
an internal operating guide, not legal advice.

## 2. Client risk rating

Compliance assigns a rating of Standard, Elevated, or High using factors such
as ownership complexity, geography, product use, transaction pattern,
politically exposed person indicators, adverse media, and source-of-funds
concerns.

## 3. High-risk client checks

Before activating a High-risk client, Compliance must complete enhanced due
diligence, including:

- Verification of identity and beneficial ownership using independent sources.
- Documented source of wealth and source of funds.
- Sanctions and politically exposed person screening.
- Adverse media review and rationale for the risk decision.
- Approval by the Director of Compliance.
- A monitoring plan with review frequency and escalation triggers.

High-risk relationships must be reviewed at least every **12 months** and
sooner when a material change occurs.

## 4. Transaction concerns

Employees must escalate unusual activity, unexplained transfers, structuring
patterns, or information that conflicts with the client profile to the
Compliance case queue. Employees must not investigate independently beyond
their assigned procedure and must not alert the client to a confidential
review.

## 5. Escalation

The Compliance analyst documents the facts and notifies the Director of
Compliance. The Director determines whether further review, account
restrictions, or a report to an appropriate authority is required. Business
teams must not override a Compliance hold.

## 6. Evidence and retention

Compliance records screening results, decisions, approvals, review dates, and
supporting evidence in the restricted case system. Access is limited to
Compliance, Legal, Internal Audit, and specifically authorized investigators.
