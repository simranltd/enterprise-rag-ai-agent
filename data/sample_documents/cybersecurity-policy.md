## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes minimum safeguards for Northstar information,
technology, and connected services. It applies to employees, contractors, and
approved third parties using Northstar systems.

## 2. Account protection

Users must use a unique account, a password that meets the corporate
standard, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) wherever it is offered.
Credentials, recovery codes, and security tokens must not be shared.

If a user loses access to MFA, the user must contact the Service Desk using
the approved support channel. The Service Desk verifies identity using two
independent checks before issuing a temporary recovery process. Users must not
ask a colleague to bypass MFA or create a shared account.

## 3. Reporting suspected incidents

Suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized access, lost devices, or accidental
disclosure must be reported immediately to the Service Desk and the Security
Operations mailbox. Users should preserve relevant messages and should not
delete or investigate suspicious artifacts beyond the safe instructions
provided by Information Security.

## 4. Secure use of information

Confidential or Restricted information may be stored only in approved
Northstar systems. Removable media requires prior Information Security
approval and encryption. Production data must not be copied into personal
accounts, public generative AI tools, or unapproved collaboration services.

## 5. Access reviews

Managers must review access attestations for their teams at least quarterly.
System owners must remove unnecessary privileges and report unresolved
exceptions to Information Security.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations may result in access suspension, investigation, disciplinary action,
contract remedies, or notification to authorities where required. Exceptions
require documented risk acceptance by the CISO.
