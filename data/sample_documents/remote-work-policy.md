## 1. Scope

Remote work is a work arrangement approved by Northstar and the employee's
manager. It is not an entitlement and may be changed when business, client,
security, or regulatory needs require it.

## 2. Working outside Canada

Employees may work temporarily from another Canadian province or territory
with manager approval and confirmation that the work location is recorded.

Employees **may not work outside Canada** while using this policy unless
People Operations, Tax, Legal, Information Security, and the employee's
manager provide written approval before travel. Approval is case-specific and
does not guarantee permission for future trips. Employees must not perform
Northstar work from a sanctioned or restricted location.

The request must identify the country, dates, duties, systems to be accessed,
and any client or data residency considerations. People Operations and Tax
assess employment, payroll, and tax implications; Information Security
assesses connectivity and data risks.

## 3. Work location and equipment

Employees must use an approved device, secure network, screen privacy, and
company-managed authentication. Restricted information must not be printed or
left visible in shared spaces. Public Wi-Fi requires an approved secure
connection.

## 4. Health, safety, and availability

The remote workspace must be reasonably safe and suitable for work.
Employees remain responsible for meeting agreed availability, attending
required meetings, and protecting confidential information.

## 5. Policy conflict

Where this policy conflicts with a specific security, tax, immigration, or
client requirement, the more restrictive requirement controls until the
responsible policy owner resolves the conflict. Questions go to People
Operations before travel.

## 6. Superseded version

Remote Work Policy version 1.4, effective 2024-05-01, allowed manager-only
approval for up to 10 business days outside Canada. That rule is superseded.
Version 2.0 requires the cross-functional written approval described above.
