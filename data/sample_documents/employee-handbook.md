## 1. Purpose and scope

This handbook describes general workplace expectations for Northstar
Financial Services employees. It applies to regular and fixed-term employees
unless a signed employment agreement or a more specific policy states
otherwise. It is guidance and does not replace applicable law.

## 2. Working hours and availability

Employees are expected to be available during the working hours agreed with
their manager and to maintain reliable contact during scheduled time. Team
members must record absences in the approved calendar and notify their
manager as soon as reasonably possible when they cannot work.

## 3. Respectful workplace

Northstar does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or
retaliation. Concerns may be raised with a manager, People Operations, or the
confidential reporting channel. Reports are handled as discreetly as
possible, subject to investigation and legal obligations.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose an actual, potential, or perceived conflict of
interest to their manager and People Operations before participating in a
related business decision. Outside employment must not interfere with
Northstar duties, use Northstar resources, or create a conflict with a
client or supplier.

## 5. Information handling

Employees must protect confidential client, employee, and company
information. Information may be accessed only for a legitimate business
purpose and must be stored in approved systems. Questions about classification
or disclosure should be directed to Information Security.

## 6. Policy hierarchy and updates

This handbook is a general reference. A specific, current policy controls
when it addresses the same topic in more detail. The published document
repository is the authoritative source for current versions. Employees must
complete required policy acknowledgements within the period communicated by
People Operations.

## 7. Leaving Northstar

On resignation or termination, employees must return company property,
protect confidential information, and cooperate with the offboarding process.
Technology access is governed by the IT Access Policy and may be removed
before the final working day when risk requires it.

## 8. Questions

Questions about employment conditions should be directed to People Operations.
This fictional handbook does not provide legal advice.
