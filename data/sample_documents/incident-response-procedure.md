## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how Northstar identifies, contains, investigates,
communicates, and closes suspected cybersecurity incidents. It applies to
employees, contractors, and technology service providers.

## 2. Incident reporting and initial triage

Anyone who suspects an incident must contact the Service Desk and Security
Operations immediately. Security Operations records the event, assigns an
initial severity, preserves available evidence, and identifies affected
systems and information.

Do not shut down or wipe a device unless instructed by Security Operations.
Do not contact an external party, client, regulator, or suspected attacker
without authorization from the Incident Commander.

## 3. Escalation process

Security Operations escalates a suspected **High** or **Critical** incident
to the Incident Commander immediately. The Incident Commander notifies the
CISO, Technology leadership, Legal, Privacy, and relevant business owners.
Compliance is included when client, AML, or regulated information may be
affected. Executive leadership determines whether crisis management is
required.

Medium incidents are managed by Security Operations with the affected system
owner and are escalated if scope or impact increases. Low events are recorded
and handled through normal service management.

## 4. Response stages

1. **Containment:** limit spread and preserve evidence.
2. **Eradication:** remove malicious access or the underlying cause.
3. **Recovery:** restore services safely and monitor for recurrence.
4. **Lessons learned:** document root cause, decisions, impact, and corrective
   actions.

## 5. Communications

Only authorized Communications, Legal, Privacy, or executive representatives
may issue external notifications. Internal updates must use approved channels
and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

## 6. Closure

The Incident Commander approves closure after recovery evidence, required
notifications, outstanding risks, and corrective actions are documented.
Security Operations retains the incident record in the restricted case system.
